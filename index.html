Quick-number memory sheet

Front tire tread: minimum 4/32 inch
Rear drive & trailer tread: minimum 2/32 inch
Belt deflection: more than 1/2–3/4 inch is excessive; belt should be snug
Slack adjuster/pushrod: about 1 inch maximum movement by hand with brakes released
Governor cutout: 120–140 PSI
Applied air-brake test: hold brake 1 minute; gauges should not drop more than 4 PSI
Low-air warning: approximately 55 PSI
Protection & parking valves pop: approximately 40 PSI
Service brake road test: 5 mph

Railroad crossing script

Approaching: traffic check front to back; train check left to right.

Crossing: “I will not brake, shift or accelerate over the track.”

After trailer clears: traffic check front to back; “I will stay in the right lane and accelerate.”

Emergency equipment — FET

Fire extinguisher securely mounted and properly charged; three reflective warning triangles; spare electrical fuses.
